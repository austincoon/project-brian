        How to play

        Be the first to bring all five marbles Home. Everyone rolls two dice to begin; the highest total goes first. Tied leaders roll again.

        01

        Get out there

        A 1 or 6 brings a marble from Base to Start. Use the other die to keep moving or release another marble if Start is clear.

        02

        Make every die count

        Use each die separately, in either order, on one or two marbles. Doubles earn another turn. Unusable dice are discarded automatically.

        03

        A little aggravation

        Land on an opponent to send them back to Base. You can pass opponents, but cannot pass or land on your own marbles.

        04

        Take a Gambit

        Land exactly on the center via a dashed route. On a later turn, a 1 or 6 exits to any corner access space. Opponents can capture you here, too.

        05

        Bring it Home

        Travel counterclockwise to your Home entrance. Move into and along your lane with an exact die value. Home is safe from capture.

        06

        Your move, your way

        Choose a ringed marble, then a highlighted destination. Or use the numbered marble and move buttons beside the board. Each move shows the die it uses.
